## 2. Fluent-reader review

Under §8.5.4, the terms were reviewed by a fluent reader and approved by the repository owner on
2026-09-21. The reader corrected the first proposal, which used Malayalam loanwords:

| Meaning | Proposed | Approved Malayalam | Approved Sanskrit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Contact / Contacts | `കോൺടാക്റ്റ്` / `കോൺടാക്റ്റുകൾ` | `വിലാസവിവരം` / `വിലാസവിവരങ്ങൾ` | `सम्पर्कः` / `सम्पर्काः` |
| Call (phone call) | `കോൾ` | `ഫോൺ വിളി` | `आह्वानम्` |
| Tag / Tags | `ടാഗ്` / `ടാഗുകൾ` | `അടയാളം` / `അടയാളങ്ങൾ` | `चिह्नम्` / `चिह्नानि` |
| Phone number | `ഫോൺ നമ്പർ` | `ഫോൺ നമ്പർ` (kept) | `दूरभाषसङ्ख्या` (kept) |

The reader gave the plural form for Contact; the owner confirmed the Malayalam singular
`വിലാസവിവരം` and the Sanskrit singular `सम्पर्कः` for single-item strings ("1 contact",
"Choose a contact").
